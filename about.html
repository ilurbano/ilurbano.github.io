About

Ivan Louise Urbano

Hello! I'm Ivan, a BSIT-MWA student at National University - Mall of Asia with a strong interest in software development, particularly mobile and web applications.

I enjoy turning ideas into functional digital solutions, from designing user-friendly interfaces to developing backend systems and integrating technologies. Through academic and personal projects, I've gained hands-on experience with application development, database management, system integration, and cloud-based services.

One of my major projects is MindGarden, a gamified student well-being platform developed for NU MOA's Guidance Services Office. As its Project Manager and Full-Stack Developer, I led a five-member team while contributing to application development, technical planning, backend integration, and deployment.

Beyond programming, I'm continuously working on improving my problem-solving, communication, leadership, and collaboration skills. I enjoy learning new technologies, exploring creative ideas, and building applications that serve practical needs.
